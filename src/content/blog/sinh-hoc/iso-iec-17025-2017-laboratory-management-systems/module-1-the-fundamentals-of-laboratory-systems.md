# 2. Aims & Objectives of ISO/IEC 17025:2017
- International Organization for Standardization (ISO) is a global federation of its member bodies (ISO member bodies). ISO technical committees participate in the preparation of standards. Several international governmental and non-governmental organizations also collaborate with ISO in preparation for these standards.
- The paradigm of this course is ISO/IEC 17025:2017, which is a standard developed for laboratory accreditation. The standard is a result of the collaboration of ISO and IEC. The current edition of ISO 17025 has replaced its second edition, i.e., ISO/IEC 17025:2005.
## The Basics of ISO/IEC 17025
- As mentioned before, ISO/IEC 17025 sets out requirements for the laboratories to operate competently and consistently. This standard provides specific activities to be included in a laboratory’s operations, such that the laboratory produces valid and reliable testing, calibration, and results.
## Aims and Objectives
- Promoting confidence in laboratories' operations
- Enabling the laboratories to operate competently
- Enabling the laboratories to generate valid results
## What does ISO/IEC 17025 require from Laboratories? 
- ISO/IEC 17025 requires laboratories to plan and implement actions for addressing risks and opportunities. Managing risks and opportunities establishes a solid basis for a highly effective management system. This, in turn, achieves improved results and prevents any adverse effects.

- ISO/IEC 17025 assists sampling, conformity, and testing in laboratories in many ways, such as
	- It harmonizes procedures
	- It harmonizes standard methods
	- It facilitates cooperation between laboratories and other bodies
	- It promotes result acceptance among countries
- Laboratories confirming to ISO/IEC 17025 will also have to operate under ISO 9001’s principles

# 3. Accrediting to ISO/IEC 17025:2017
## Benefit of Accreditation of ISO/IEC 17025:2016
![Ảnh chụp Màn hình 2026-07-11 lúc 23.01.16](/vault-assets/anh-chup-man-hinh-2026-07-11-luc-23-01-16-fac8fa0a.png)
- **Increasing Customer Confidence from ISO/IEC 17025 Accreditation: **
	- By accrediting to ISO/IEC 17025, your laboratory will demonstrate that  
		- (1) It is capable of providing valid results with consistency  
		- (2)All of the individuals working for it are competent  
		- (3) All of its accredited measurement results can be cross-checked with the International System of Units (SI) or any other appropriate references  
	- By fulfilling these above-mentioned primary objectives, your laboratory’s results can be readily accepted among other countries.
- **Proactive Risk-Based Approach:** Pre-defined quality objectives, policies, and activities are the key elements that provide a strategic direction to any organization. A risk-based thinking culture makes executing cost-effective operations and evidence-based decision-making possible. By accrediting your laboratory to ISO/IE 17025, you ensure that all of the significant quality-related risks to tests and calibrations are already known and well-controlled, i.e., they are executed the same way every time.
- Assuring the Laboratory's Credibility: The testing and calibration methods of your laboratory must be reviewed and audited. This ensures that you are using the latest available technology and documentation. A third-party accreditation body performs an assessment and verifies that all the tests and calibrations are done correctly by trained professionals.
- **Creation of a Professional Environment:** A third-party assessment allows you to feel a sense of accomplishment, and it will enable you to take steps through which you can create a professional environment.
## Steps of Getting  ISO/IEC 17025 Accreditation
- **Step 1: Have a copy of ISO/IEC 17025:2017**
	- Provide a copy of the ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and gain an understanding of this standard. Hire an ISO 17025 accreditation body and fulfill their requirements.   
	- For getting accredited, it is a must for all of your employees to have some training, especially those responsible for management activities and technical activities. This training is also required for those employees who act as internal auditors.
- **Step 2: Define and document the laboratory activities that require accreditation**: 
	- The next step in the accreditation process is defining and documenting the laboratory activities for which you seek accreditation. In this step, you should also document and describe the quality objectives of your laboratory. By doing this, you will be able to get a context for determining the extent to which you can control the processes and document them.
- **Step 3: Perform a GAP analysis: **
	- After completing steps 1 and 2, perform a GAP analysis to determine what your laboratory is doing and what are the ISO/IEC 17025’s requirements.
- **Step 4: Perform documentation:**
	- A documentation manual, although not required, allows your laboratory to document its policies and objectives in one place. Several procedures are needed for documentation, and you must link together, keep, and maintain a number of records.
## Application Process of ISO/IEC 17025
- Before applying for accreditation, your laboratory should meet the accreditation body’s requirements. It is also necessary for you to have
	- A documentation of all the mandatory procedures
	- The records for showing the completion of the accreditation process
	- An active internal audit program, and
	- The records regarding management reviews method validation
- After performing a remote review of all the documentation and activities, your accreditation body will approve recommendations and schedule your accreditation audit. Before recommending your laboratory for accreditation, it will be optimum for you to
	- Have an activity period worth six months, and
	- Record-keeping (objective evidence) for satisfying your assessors
## Training Options & Recommendation for Individuals
- **Additional Process & Management Requirements Training:** This training includes options for addressing risks and opportunities, corrective action, cause analysis, and effective nonconforming specifications. It also trains individuals in data control and information management (computer systems validation).
- **Internal Auditor Training:** Training in accordance with ISO 19011 is necessary for internal auditors. These internal auditors must be familiar with the standard, as well as the method of conducting the audit. For an individual, the certification is possible by participating in the ISO 17025 Lead Auditors Course, which is certified by CQI’s (Chartered Quality Institute) International Register of Certified Auditors (ICRA).
- **Technical Training:** This training enables the individuals with method validation and evaluation of measurement uncertainty and quality control. For the personnel responsible for reporting the quality results, it is necessary to have some formal training in this regard.
- **ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Familiarization Training:** Prior to the implementation of an ISO/IEC 17025, the teams responsible for implementation should make themselves completely familiar with all of the subject matter given in the standard. They should understand the standard's intent and each of its clauses, along with the required documentation. This training should help them with a processed approach to complete the activities, risk-based thinking, and evidence-based decision making. For the rest of the laboratory employees, it is necessary to understand the standard. They can equip themselves further in this regard over time.

# 4. Main Sections of ISO/IEC 17025:2017
## Section 4: General Requirements
- Section 4 of ISO/IEC 17025 covers the two main requirements, i.e.
	- **Impartiality**: Impartiality implies that the laboratory will not permit any commercial, financial, or other pressures to put the quality of its results at stake. It also means that any internal issues, personal relationships, and conflicts of interest are thoroughly addressed and resolved.
	- **Confidentiality**: It requires the laboratories to keep all of their results and information private.
- Both of these requirements are important for maintaining trust and confidence in the users of tests and calibrations of the laboratories. (→ [Prepare Data for Exploration](/blog/sinh-hoc/bioinformatics/b-data-science-and-bioinformatics/i-data-science/03-prepare-data-for-exploration/))
## Section 5: Structural Requirements
- Points Defined for a Laboratory in Section 5 of ISO/IEC 17025:2017
	- Basic organizational components
	- Range of activities
	- Commitment to be a part of an effective management system
- This section defines the basic requirements for
	- Personnel
	- The authority they have, and
	- The required resources for executing their duties
- What other requirements are Set forward by Section 5: 
	- **Condition to be a Legal Entity or part of a Legal Entity:** Section 5 also puts forward the condition for an accredited laboratory to be a legal entity or be a part of a legal entity. This legal entity will be responsible for the testing and calibration activities of the laboratory.
	- **Management Responsibilities: **Section 5 also puts forward the management responsibilities in an accredited laboratory along with its responsibilities to customers, regulatory authorities, and organizations providing recognition.
## Section 6: Resource Requirements
- The Factors Addressed by Section 6: 
	- Facilities for Laboratories
	- Personnel for Laboratories
	- Equipment System for Laboratories
	- Support Systems for Laboratories
## Section 7: Process Requirements
- In this section, eleven main processes for improving efficiency are discussed. This section begins with the **Review of Requests, Tenders, and Contracts.**
### What is the Most Important Part of this Section?
- The most important part of this section in particular and the whole standard, in general, is the **Selection, Verification, and Validation of Methods.** In this section, the following are covered
	- Sampling
	- The handling of the test items, and
	- Technical record keeping
- The quality monitoring and control function are used for ensuring the validity of the laboratory’s results. For this purpose, several monitoring tools are listed in this section, along with the requirements for proficiency testing.
## Section 8: Management System Requirements
- Policies and objectives
- Documentation control
- Record control
- Addressing risks and opportunities
- Bringing out improvement, and
- Taking corrective action
Section 8 ends with the internal audit and management review. Since ISO.IEC 17025 provides the general requirements only, it is best for the laboratories to consult ISO 19011 regarding the guidelines for auditing management systems, for filling in the details of internal audits.
# 5. Lesson Summary
- Aims & Objectives of ISO/IEC 17025
	- Promoting confidence in laboratories’ operations
	- Enabling the laboratories to operate competently
	- Enabling laboratories such that they generate valid results
- Types of Laboratories
	- Testing Laboratories: They determine the characteristics of an item of interest for performing conformity assessment
	- Calibration Laboratories: They compare a measurement apparatus with an unknown accuracy with another apparatus of known accuracy
- Verbal Forms Used Throughout ISO/IEC 17025
	- Any **requirement** is indicated by **"shall"**
	- Any **recommendation** is indicated by **"should"**
	- A **permission** is indicated by **"may"**
	- A **possibility** or a capability is shown by **"can"**
- What Changes Have Been Made in the Current Edition of ISO/IEC 17025?
	- **Risk-based thinking** in the current edition has allowed **reduction in perspective requirements**
	- **Risk-based thinking** has also enabled the **replacement of perspective requirements** by p**erformance-based requirements**
	- The current edition of ISO/IEC 17025 is more **flexible regarding requirements for processes, procedures, documented information, and organizational responsibilities**
	- The current version of ISO/IEC 17025 has **also included a definition of "laboratory" in its documents**
- Benefits of ISO/IEC 17025 Accreditation
	- It **increases customer confidence**
	- It **allows a proactive risk-based approach**
	- It **assures the laboratory's creditability**
	- It **creates** **a** **professional** **environment**
- Steps of Getting ISO/IEC 17025 Accreditation
	- Step 1 – Have a copy of ISO/IEC 17025:2017 standard
	- Step 2 – Defining and documenting the laboratory activities
	- Step 3 – Performing a GAP analysis
	- Step 4 – Performing documentation
- - Section 4 of ISO 17025 defines **general requirements**. Its two main components are **impartiality and confidentiality**
- Section 5 defines **structural requirements** and **basic organizational components,** **range of activities,** and **commitment to being a part of an effective management system**
- Section 6 defines **structural requirements** regarding the **availability of facilities, personnel, equipment system, and support system for the laboratories**
- Section 7 is dedicated to **processing requirements.** The most important part of this section in particular and whole standard, in general, is the **Selection, Verification, and Validation of Methods**
- Section 8 is for defining **management requirements** for the laboratories
