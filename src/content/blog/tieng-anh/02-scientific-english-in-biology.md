## Homework 4 – Writing (11/09/2023)
- Đề: viết một bản tin ngắn tối đa 10 câu về chủ đề khoa học bất kỳ; viết tay; nộp Google Classroom; không đạo văn.

### The Origin of Mitochondria
Mitochondria, also known as "the powerhouse of the cell", play a crucial role in cellular respiration, which is a process that converts sugar into energy. They are essential for most eukaryotic organisms and can be found in the cytoplasm of nearly every cell. And the presence of DNA within the mitochondria has raised questions among scientists about its formation. One of the most well-known theories suggests that Mitochondria were once prokaryotes. Specifically, the α-proteobacteria, a bacteria phylum, show the closest identified relation to mitochondria. Multiple pieces of evidence support the idea that mitochondria originated from an endosymbiotic event. The inner membrane of mitochondria contains significant foldings called cristae, which resemble the outer surface of alpha-proteobacteria. These cristae are enriched with enzymes necessary for aerobic respiration. Mitochondria possess their own circular DNA chromosome, which is anchored to the inner membrane and contains genes similar to genes expressed by alpha-proteobacteria. Mitochondria do not freely arise within the eukaryotic cells; they replicate internally and are divided between two cells during cell division. Therefore, while mitochondria are highly integrated into the eukaryotic cell, they reproduce as if they were independent organisms within the cell. Additionally, Mitochondria have special ribosomes and transfer RNAs that resemble the components found in prokaryotes. These features all support the notion that mitochondria were once free-living prokaryotes.

- Kiến thức liên quan: **thuyết nội cộng sinh (endosymbiotic theory)** – DNA vòng, ribosome 70S, nhân đôi độc lập (→ [Bào quan – ti thể](/blog/te-bao-chat-bao-quan-va-nhan-te-bao/), [Di truyền ngoài nhân](/blog/di-truyen-ngoai-nhan-moi-truong-va-bien-di/))

- Đề giới hạn **10 câu**; bài hiện có 13 câu → có thể gộp câu 3–4 và câu 9–10.

- Viết thường "mitochondria" khi không đứng đầu câu; tránh mở câu bằng "And".
- "resemble the outer surface of alpha-proteobacteria" → chính xác hơn: *resemble the internal membranes of some α-proteobacteria*.

## Homework 5 – Recording (18/09/2023)
- Đề: đóng vai phóng viên/người dẫn bản tin, trình bày một tin khoa học hoặc giải thích một chủ đề sinh học trong 4–5 phút; quay video có mặt; cấu trúc Introduction – Main body – Concluding statement; phát âm chuẩn; sáng tạo; slide tuỳ chọn.

### Script: Egg turning and embryo positioning in snakes
Hello, everyone. Welcome to my first vlog. First of all, I would like to take a moment to introduce myself. I'm Nguyễn Thị Thu Hà. You can call me Thu Hà. I am currently studying for my Bachelor's degree in Biology at Hanoi University of Science. And today, in this video, I will talk about how egg turning and embryo positioning affects hatching success in snakes. But before we start, I want to introduce you guys to a video.

So, as you can see in the video, they did two things. First, they removed the female snake from her eggs. Second, they marked the top of the eggs with a pen before transferring them to an incubator.

To find an explanation for these questions, I conducted some research. I discovered that the snake in the video is a Python snake. Unlike almost every other snake, which leaves their nesting site within hours after laying eggs, Pythons are an exception and stay with their eggs until the incubation process is complete. The average incubation period for a snake is 55-60 days. However, during this period, the mother might be at risk of getting killed because she wraps herself around the eggs and refrains from consuming any food or water. Therefore, the eggs need to be removed and transferred to a new clutch to increase their survival rate.

Second, the person in the video marks the top of the eggs with an "X" before placing them in an incubator. This practice is based on a common belief among amateur and professional reptile breeders. According to this belief, freshly laid eggs should be marked with a pen or pencil to distinguish the top from the bottom before being placed in an incubator. However, I found an article that addresses this issue and provides some insights. The results of the research indicate the following:

1. Top embryo eggs did not exhibit better survival rates compared to bottom embryo eggs. Hatchlings from both categories had similar survival rates. However, top embryo eggs experienced greater mass loss throughout the incubation period, hatched earlier, and produced lighter and shorter snakes at birth than bottom embryo eggs.
2. The research does not support the common belief that egg turning in reptiles can kill or significantly affect the developing embryo, at least when the eggs are turned within 12 hours after being laid.
