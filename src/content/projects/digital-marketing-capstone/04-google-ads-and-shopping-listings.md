## Google Shopping: listing optimization
Products are synced from Shopify to Google Merchant Center. Rules: the title starts with what the product is and the words people search, the first image is the lamp on a plain background, every attribute is filled.

| Field | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | "The Maestro" | "Upcycled Trumpet Table Lamp – Brass, Handmade, LED Bulb Included" |
| Description | "One of a kind." | "Handmade table lamp built from a vintage brass trumpet. Rewired and safety tested, 45 cm tall, warm LED bulb included. 5% of each sale supports desert clean-ups." |
| Product type / Google category | – | Home & Garden > Lighting > Lamps |
| Attributes | missing | color: brass · material: recycled metal · condition: refurbished *(only if the item meets Google's definition; otherwise new)* · shipping weight |
| Image | lamp in a dark room | lamp on a white background; lifestyle image as additional image |
| Price | $189 | $189, with sale price during launch week |

**Campaign:** Performance Max for Shopping, $450/month, target ROAS 300% *(assumption)* after two weeks of learning. Exclude search terms like "lamp repair", "trumpet for sale".

## Google Ads: search campaign ($150/month)
**Ad groups and keywords** (phrase and exact match)
- Brand: "sinclair verde", "sinclair verde lamps"
- Upcycled lighting: "upcycled lamp", "recycled lamp", "repurposed lamp"
- Unique decor: "unique table lamp", "handmade lamp"

**Negative keywords:** "diy", "how to make", "lamp parts", "free" (lesson from the ShearShare case: broad terms bring unqualified traffic).

**Responsive search ad, upcycled lighting**
- Headlines: Upcycled Lamps, Handmade in Phoenix · One-of-a-Kind Table Lamps · Lamps That Had a Past Life · 5% of Every Sale to Clean-Ups · Rewired, Tested, Ready to Ship
- Descriptions: Each lamp starts life as something else: a trumpet, a bike wheel, a globe. Shop unique lighting today. · Sustainable decor at fair prices. Free shipping over $120 *(assumption)*.
- Final URL: collection page with UTM `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=upcycled`.
- Extensions: sitelinks (Table lamps, Pendant lights, Our story, Shipping), callout (Handmade · Safety tested).

## What to check weekly
Search terms report (add negatives), CTR by ad group, cost per conversion, Merchant Center disapprovals.
