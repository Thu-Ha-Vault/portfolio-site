## "Second Life Club" loyalty program
Lamps are not bought every month, so the program rewards **referrals and engagement**, not only repeat purchases, and fits the sustainability story.

| Action | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Join the club (free, email sign-up) | early access to new pieces (24 h before everyone else) |
| First purchase | 100 points (= $10 off the next order) |
| Refer a friend who buys | friend gets 10% off; member gets 200 points |
| Share a photo of the lamp at home (#UpcycledLight) | 50 points, chance to be featured |
| Donate an object for upcycling at the Phoenix store | 150 points + the object's story on the lamp tag |
| Birthday | double points for the month |

**Tiers:** Spark (0–299 points), Glow (300–799, free shipping), Radiance (800+, first pick of commissioned pieces).
**Tool:** a Shopify loyalty app; points shown on the customer account page.

## Post-purchase follow-up
| When | Message | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | order confirmation + the lamp's origin story card | delight, reduce "looks like the photo?" worries |
| Day 2 after delivery | setup and care tips (bulb type, cleaning brass) | fewer returns, support |
| Day 7 | 3-question survey (below) | satisfaction data |
| Day 14 | review request + photo-sharing invitation (50 points) | reviews, UGC |
| Day 45 | "Second Life Club" referral reminder | referrals |

## Survey (day 7)
1. How satisfied are you with your lamp? *(1–5)*
2. How likely are you to recommend Sinclair Verde to a friend? *(0–10, Net Promoter Score)*
3. What almost stopped you from buying? *(price / shipping cost / unsure about size / unsure about quality / nothing / other)*

**Measures:** CSAT (share of 4–5), NPS (promoters − detractors), top purchase barrier → feeds product pages (add size photos with a hand for scale if "unsure about size" leads).

## Customer service
FAQ page (shipping, bulb types, safety testing, returns) to save time on common questions and appear in Google's featured snippets; replies to messages within one business day.
