## Measurement plan
| Question | Metric | Source | Target (month 1) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Are we reaching the right people? | reach, impressions in the US/CA 30–55 audience | X, YouTube analytics | 50,000 reach |
| Is the content worth sharing? | share rate, key hashtag reach | X analytics | share rate ≥ 1.5% |
| Do people come to the store? | link clicks, sessions by source | Google Analytics (UTM) | 4,000 sessions |
| Do visitors become leads? | email sign-ups / sessions | Shopify, GA | 8% |
| **Do leads buy? (KPI)** | lead conversion % (subscriber → first order) | Shopify Email | 5% |
| **New customers (KPI)** | first-time buyers online; share vs store | Shopify customer reports, store POS | 3× the store's first-time share |
| Is spend efficient? | CPA, ROAS by channel | Google Ads, GA | CPA ≤ $25, ROAS ≥ 300% |

Conversions set up in Google Analytics: `sign_up`, `begin_checkout`, `purchase` (with value). All links carry UTM tags.

## First-month report *(illustrative data)*
| Channel | Spend | Sessions | Orders | Revenue | ROAS |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Google Shopping | $450 | 1,620 | 41 | $5,330 | 1,184% |
| Google Search | $150 | 540 | 12 | $1,500 | 1,000% |
| YouTube | $200 | 980 | 6 | $750 | 375% |
| X (organic + $100 promoted) | $100 | 760 | 5 | $610 | 610% |
| Email | $100 | 610 | 18 | $2,190 | 2,190% |
| **Total** | **$1,000** | **4,510** | **82** | **$10,380** | **1,038%** |

- Email sign-ups: 410 (9.1% of sessions); lead conversion: 4.4% (goal 5%).
- New customers: 68 of 82 orders (83%) were first-time buyers.
- Best organic post: the trumpet before/after (share rate 3.2%).

## Insights and next steps
1. **Shopping is the engine**: highest orders and ROAS → move $100 from X promotion to Shopping in month 2.
2. **YouTube builds awareness but converts slowly**: keep it, but add a product link card and retarget viewers with Shopping ads.
3. **Lead conversion is just below goal**: test a stronger email 3 (free shipping instead of a reminder) and add the welcome code to the sign-up pop-up.
4. **Content that shows the past life of the object performs best**: make it the first post of every week.
