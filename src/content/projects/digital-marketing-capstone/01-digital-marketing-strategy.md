> Prepared as an entry-level strategist at **Good Mercury Marketing**. Brief from the course; prices, donation share and benchmark rates are assumptions, marked *(assumption)*.

## The business
Sinclair Verde, an in-store furniture retailer in Phoenix, Arizona, turns unwanted products into lamps (**upcycling**) and donates part of each sale to environmental non-profits. It has just opened an online store on Shopify. Lamps sell for $79–$189 *(assumption)*; 5% of each sale goes to the non-profit partner *(assumption)*.

## Objectives
| Business objective | SMART marketing goal (first 3 months after launch) |
| --- | --- |
| Brand awareness | Reach 150,000 people in the target market on X and YouTube and grow the email list to 1,500 subscribers |
| Online sales | Make first-time online buyers **3×** the share of first-time buyers in the store (the brief's desired outcome); 250 online orders, at least 60% from new customers |
| Google Shopping | All 40 products approved and live in Google Shopping in week 1; Shopping click-through rate ≥ 1% |

## Customer persona: Tanya Ricoma
- 36, mother of two, San Francisco; cares about recycling and reducing waste.
- Hobbies: interior decorating, collecting creative art on a budget.
- Shops on her phone in the evening; looks for unique pieces with a story; compares prices before buying.
- **What she needs to hear:** one-of-a-kind design, a clear sustainability story (what the lamp was before), a fair price, and safe shipping.
- **Barriers:** "Will it look like the photo?", "Is the wiring safe?", shipping cost.

## Target market
Adults 30–55 in the US and Canada who care about home decor and style; their social media activity rose at the start of the pandemic. Secondary: Phoenix locals who know the store (retargeting).

## Channels and roles
| Channel | Role in the funnel | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | awareness | 60-second "before and after" upcycling videos, Shorts |
| X | awareness, community | progress photos, design threads, #UpcycledLight |
| Google Shopping + Google Ads | consideration, conversion | product listings, branded search campaign |
| Email (Shopify Email) | conversion, loyalty | welcome series, launch offer, post-purchase follow-up |
| Shopify store | conversion | product pages with the lamp's origin story, reviews |
| Google Analytics | measurement | traffic sources, conversion, new vs returning buyers |

## Budget: $1,000 per month
| Item | Monthly | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Google Shopping ads | $450 | closest to purchase; product images sell the design |
| Google Ads (search, brand + "upcycled lamp" phrases) | $150 | captures people already searching |
| YouTube video ads (in-feed) | $200 | awareness with the before/after videos |
| X promoted posts | $100 | boost the best organic posts |
| Email + creative tools | $100 | Shopify Email, design templates |

## KPIs and metrics
- **KPIs:** lead conversion % (subscriber → first order), number of new customers.
- **Metrics to watch:** share rate, reach of key posts and #UpcycledLight, link clicks, Shopping CTR, cost per acquisition, return on ad spend.

## Launch timeline
| Week | Pre-launch / launch / post-launch |
| --- | --- |
| −2 to −1 | listings to Google Merchant Center, teaser posts, waitlist sign-up page |
| 0 | launch email, YouTube video, Shopping and search campaigns live |
| 1–4 | weekly content, A/B tests on email subject lines and ad images |
| 5–12 | optimize budget toward best channels, loyalty program, monthly report |
