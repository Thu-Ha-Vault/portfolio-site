**Trigger:** someone joins the list (waitlist page, pop-up on the store, in-store QR code). **Goal:** first online order (KPI: lead conversion %). **Segments:** new subscribers who have not bought yet; buyers leave the series after a purchase.

## Email 1: welcome (sent immediately)
- **Subject A:** Welcome to Sinclair Verde: lamps with a past
- **Subject B:** Your first look at our upcycled lamps (+10% inside)
- **Preview text:** Every lamp started life as something else.
- **Body:** Thanks for joining. We turn unwanted objects into one-of-a-kind lamps in our Phoenix workshop, and part of every sale funds local environmental groups. Here are three favourites and the story behind each. As a welcome, use **WELCOME10** for 10% off your first order this week.
- **CTA:** Shop the collection

## Email 2: the story (day 3)
- **Subject:** What happens to a trumpet nobody plays?
- **Body:** A short before-and-after story with the YouTube film, safety note (every lamp is rewired and tested), and how we pack lamps for shipping.
- **CTA:** Watch the 60-second film / Find your lamp

## Email 3: social proof and reminder (day 6)
- **Subject:** Your 10% ends tomorrow
- **Body:** Two customer photos, a review, free shipping threshold, reminder that each piece is one of a kind.
- **CTA:** Use WELCOME10 before it expires

## Testing and measurement
- A/B test the subject line of email 1 (A vs B, 50/50 on the first 400 subscribers; winner by open rate, then click rate).
- Track opens, click-through rate, unsubscribe rate and first orders from the series (UTM `utm_source=email&utm_campaign=welcome`).
- Benchmarks to beat *(assumption)*: open rate 40%, click rate 4%, series conversion 5%.

## Compliance
Sender address and unsubscribe link in every email; consent recorded at sign-up (CAN-SPAM for the US, CASL for Canada).
