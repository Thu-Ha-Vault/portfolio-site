## Negotiation: an out-of-scope request
The director of operations asked to add **online ordering** to the pilot. It would add an integration, extend the timeline by about six weeks and cost more than the contingency. Approach:
1. **Separate the person from the problem**: the request comes from a real goal (more orders), not opposition.
2. **Understand motivations**: the director wants proof that digital ordering raises revenue.
3. **Find mutual benefit**: the pilot already collects ordering data; the evaluation can include a recommendation on online ordering as phase 2.
4. **Build a coalition**: the finance lead (high power, budget concern) and the two general managers (high interest, staff capacity) both benefit from a focused pilot.

## Coalition email
**To:** Finance lead; General managers, pilot locations
**Subject:** Keeping the tablet pilot on time and on budget — your input by Friday

Hi all,

The director of operations has proposed adding online ordering to the tablet pilot. It is a strong idea for growth, but adding it now would push launch back by about six weeks and exceed our contingency budget, which puts the 10% faster checkout goal for this quarter at risk.

I'd like us to propose a two-step plan instead: launch the in-restaurant tablets as chartered, and use the six weeks of pilot data to build the case for online ordering as phase 2. That keeps the budget fixed for finance and protects the general managers from a second change while staff are still learning the tablets.

Could you join a 20-minute call on Thursday to agree on this proposal before I share it with the director? If you have concerns, reply here and I'll include them.

Thank you,
Peta
Project Manager, Tablet Pilot
