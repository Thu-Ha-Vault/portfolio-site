**To:** Owner / CEO (project sponsor)
**Cc:** Director of operations
**Subject:** Decision needed by Wednesday: tablet freezes are hurting guest experience at both pilot locations

Hi [Sponsor],

Tablet freezes are causing lost orders at both pilot restaurants, and they put our objective to **prioritize our customer needs** at risk: 36% of surveyed guests said the tablets made their visit worse, and freezes are the most common reason they give.

**What is happening.** Since launch, tablets freeze during peak hours about 8 times per service *(illustrative)*. Servers re-enter the order by hand, which adds wait time and undoes part of the 12% faster checkout we have achieved.

**What we have done.** We set up an issue log with the vendor, added a greeting and demo for every new table, and re-trained servers. These steps reduce confusion but do not fix the freezes.

**Options**
1. **Vendor software patch (recommended):** delivered in 5 working days, costs about $1,800 from our contingency budget, no change to the launch scope.
2. **Replace 20 tablets with the vendor's newer model:** fixes the hardware side if the patch fails, about $8,000, 3 weeks.
3. **Pause tablets at peak hours** until fixed: no cost, but we lose most of the speed gains and the pilot data for that period.

**Decision needed.** Please approve option 1 by **Wednesday** so the patch is live before the weekend. If the patch does not cut freezes by at least 80% in one week, I will come back with option 2.

Happy to walk you through it in 10 minutes if that is easier.

Best,
Peta
Project Manager, Tablet Pilot
