**Attendees:** project manager, general managers and kitchen managers of both pilot locations, a front-of-house lead from each location, tablet vendor representative.
**Ground rules:** focus on the process, not the person; everyone speaks once before anyone speaks twice.

## What went well
- Launch milestone reached on time; both locations went live the same week.
- Installation in closed hours meant no disruption to guests.
- Staff compliance above 90% by week 2; front-of-house leads coached peers on the floor.
- Positive note from the sponsor on faster checkout.

## What could have gone better
- **Delivery delay:** a paperwork error on the project manager's side slowed tablet delivery by **two business days** (opened by the project manager to model honesty).
- **Kitchen managers felt left out** of decisions made by the general managers (ticket layout, timing of the switch-over).
- Guests were not shown how to use the tablets; 36% reported a negative experience.
- The freeze bug was reported informally for a week before reaching the vendor.

## Root causes (the team's own role)
- No single owner for vendor paperwork → delay.
- Kitchen managers were not in the weekly check-in, so their input arrived late.
- Training covered staff tasks but not how staff should introduce the tablet to guests.
- No defined channel for reporting tablet issues.

## Action items (SMART)
| Action | Owner | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assign one owner for all vendor paperwork; checklist before each order | Project manager | next order |
| Invite kitchen managers to the weekly staff check-in, with a **five-minute agenda item** for their issues | General managers | from next week |
| Add a 15-second guest demo to the service steps; refresh training for all servers | General managers, training vendor | within 1 week |
| Shared issue log for tablet problems; vendor reviews it every 48 hours | Vendor representative | within 3 days |
| Re-check guest survey: negative experience below 15% | Project manager | in 2 weeks |
