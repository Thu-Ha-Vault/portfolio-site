## Milestones
| Milestone | After task | Why stakeholders care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vendor contract signed | 2.3 | sets most of the budget (finance) |
| First internal test run of ordering | 3.4 | first point to evaluate the work |
| Marketing materials approved | 6.2 | brand and guest communication (sponsor, marketing) |
| Launch at both locations | 7.1 | start of the six-week measurement |
| Evaluation and recommendation | 7.3 | basis for the chain-wide decision |

## Risks behind the low-confidence estimates
| Task | Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.4 Tablet delivery | supplier delay (a paperwork error once delayed delivery two days) | assign one owner for vendor paperwork; order 5% spare units |
| 3.2 POS integration | POS API limits | vendor proof of concept before contract signature |
| 5.2 Training | staff absences, equipment glitches | two sessions per group; recorded walkthrough |

## Negotiating estimates
Two estimates came back longer than the schedule allowed (delivery, integration). They were discussed with the task experts, not pushed down: the vendor confirmed 15 days as the most likely delivery time, so installation was moved to closed hours on two consecutive days to protect the launch date instead.
