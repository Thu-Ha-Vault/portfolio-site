## Quality standards
Sources: the charter (goals, scope), conversations with the training vendor, the tablet sales representative and the funding stakeholders. Vague wishes such as "easy to use" were turned into signs that can be checked.

| Area | Standard | How it is checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ease of use | A guest places an order in **≤ 20 seconds** after choosing items | timed test with 20 guests per location in week 1 |
| Reliability | Battery lasts a full service day (10 h) on one charge; < 1% failed orders | vendor spec, POS error log |
| Speed | Checkout time down 10% vs the six weeks before launch | POS timestamps |
| Accuracy | ≥ 98% of orders need no correction | kitchen remake log |
| Staff | **> 90%** compliance with the tablet ordering process | manager spot checks |
| Training | Each FOH server completes an order, modifier and split bill unaided; BOH reads tablet tickets correctly | sign-off checklist after the 6-hour session |
| Guest experience | Guest satisfaction ≥ 4.2 / 5; returning guests say ordering is faster | guest survey |
| Durability | Tablets expected to last 3 years before replacement (funding stakeholders) | vendor warranty, damage log |

## Evaluation questions and indicators
The milestone evaluated: **launch testing** (tablets installed, integrated with the POS system, staff trained).

| Evaluation question | Indicators |
| --- | --- |
| To what extent do tablets improve the staff's work performance? | table turnover at peak; side work completed per shift; fewer staff waiting by the beverage station; > 90% compliance |
| To what extent do tablets improve the guest's dining experience? | satisfaction score; share of guests reporting a negative experience; average checkout time |
| To what extent do tablets improve order accuracy? | remakes per 100 orders; kitchen manager feedback |
| To what extent does the business benefit? | covers per peak hour; average ticket; tip averages |

## Staff survey (front and back of house)
1. The tablets are easy to use during a busy shift. *(1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree)*
2. There was enough time during training to practise. *(1–5)*
3. Since launch, I complete my side work during the shift… *(less often / about the same / more often)*
4. How often do you need to fix an order entered on a tablet? *(never / 1–2 times per shift / 3–5 / more than 5)*
5. Which tasks take less time now? *(select all: taking orders, drink refills, payment, answering menu questions, none)*
6. What would make the tablets work better for you? *(open text)*

## Guest survey (QR code on the receipt)
1. How often do you dine at this location each month? *(0–1 / 2–3 / 4–5 / more than 5)*
2. How satisfied were you with your visit today? *(1–5)*
3. Ordering on the tablet was… *(very easy / easy / neutral / difficult / very difficult)*
4. Compared with your last visit, paying was… *(faster / about the same / slower / first visit)*
5. Did the tablet make your dining experience better or worse? *(better / no difference / worse)*
6. Anything we should change? *(open text)*

## Process
Surveys run for six weeks after launch. Results are reviewed weekly by the project manager and the two general managers; any standard missed two weeks in a row becomes an action item with an owner and a due date.
